Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

🔴 Zero-Tolerance Policy Declaration

Chana maintains an absolute, non-negotiable zero-tolerance policy against the creation, distribution, promotion, or facilitation of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or any form of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). We actively report offenders to global law enforcement and child safety authorities.

Last Updated: May 17, 2026

As a premium social discovery and matching platform, Chana is dedicated to maintaining a safe, secure, and respectful community. In accordance with regional and global legal regulations, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) guidelines and Google Play Store policies, we enforce the following strict standards.

1. Strict Age Eligibility

Chana is designed exclusively for adults. Under no circumstances is anyone under the age of 18 permitted to create an account, access the application, or interact within the Chana social environment.

  • Onboarding Verification: All users are required to submit their date of birth during registration, and our onboarding flow strictly blocks any user who indicates they are under 18 years of age.
  • Active Monitoring: Profiles suspected of belonging to individuals under 18 are immediately suspended and investigated.

2. Monitoring and Prevention Mechanisms

We deploy continuous multi-layered processes to identify, block, and eliminate any harmful or exploitative content:

  • Automated Scanning: All profile media, uploads, and texts are processed through automated filtering algorithms to intercept explicit or suspicious material.
  • Row Level Security (RLS): Our database is structured using protected tables, restricting media access to verified user sessions to block unauthorized scraping or malicious activities.
  • Manual Review: Our administrative and moderation teams conduct reviews of reported profile assets to ensure strict compliance with child safety policies.

3. Instant In-App Reporting

We empower our community to act immediately. Every user has direct access to high-priority reporting mechanisms:

  • Profile Reporting: Users can instantly report any profile violating safety standards directly inside the profile detail screens.
  • Immediate Isolation: Once a report is submitted, the reported account is isolated from the matching pool pending swift moderator review.

4. Law Enforcement & NCMEC Reporting

Chana is fully integrated with global safety networks. In the event that any attempt to upload, distribute, or share CSAM or CSAE material is detected:

  • Immediate Accounts Block: The offending user's account is permanently banned, and all associated devices are blacklisted from registering again.
  • NCMEC Submission: A formal cybertipline report containing all necessary user metadata, IP addresses, and media hashes is instantly submitted to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
  • Law Enforcement Cooperation: We cooperate fully with local and international law enforcement agencies to assist in the prosecution of child exploitation offenses.

5. Continuous Policy Audits

Our security structures are regularly audited to keep pace with changing threat landscapes and to implement advanced safety practices. We are committed to maintaining a platform that protects our community and strictly upholds the rights and safety of minors globally.

If you observe any behavior or content on our platform that raises concerns regarding minor safety, please contact our emergency safety team immediately at:
