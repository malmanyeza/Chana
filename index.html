Welcome to Chana! We are fully committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe, respectful social environment. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with a premium matching and social discovery experience, we collect the following information:

  • Profile Details: Your name, birth date, gender, preferences, country of origin, and bio.
  • Media Assets: The photos you choose to upload to complete your profile card.
  • Account Information: Email address and authentication credentials securely linked to your account.
  • App Usage Data: Swipe activities (likes, passes, superlikes) and conversation interactions inside active match chatrooms.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information to:

  • Create and manage your user account.
  • Enable discoverability, profile suggestions, and matching algorithms based on your preferences.
  • Facilitate instant realtime messaging and push notifications between you and your matches.
  • Enforce our safety standards and block abusive or fraudulent activities.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. Your profile details (name, photos, bio, interest tags, and country flag) are visible to other logged-in users of the Chana app for social matching. We do not disclose private swiping decisions (such as passes) or chat messages to third parties, except as required by law.

4. Security and Storage

Your security is of paramount importance to us. We secure your credentials using encrypted tokens (`SecureStore`) and protect your backend access using strict Row Level Security (RLS) tables inside our secure database environment.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You can view, edit, or update your profile details and photos directly inside the app's *Edit Profile* screen. You can also delete your account at any time via the *Settings* menu, which will wipe your active swiping and profile history from our live matching system.
