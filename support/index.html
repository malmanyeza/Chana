Frequently Asked Questions

How does matching work on Chana? +
Chana is built on a mutual-opt-in matching engine. You can browse through profile cards in your local area. If you are interested in connecting with a profile, swipe right. If not, swipe left. A match is successfully established only when both users have swiped right on each other, after which an instant real-time chat interface will become available.
How do I delete my Chana account and personal data? +
Your data privacy is fully respected. To permanently delete your profile, swipe history, chats, and personal details: Open the Chana app, navigate to the Settings tab, tap on Profile Settings, and select the Delete Account option. This action is permanent and instantly wipes your data from our active matching server.
What is the Likes tab? +
The Likes tab is a premium feature that shows you a list of active users who have already swiped right on your profile. This allows you to review interested profiles and immediately create matches without having to search for them in the general discovery pool.
How do I report safety concerns or abusive behavior? +
We maintain a zero-tolerance policy for harassment or safety violations. You can review safety rules inside the in-app Safety Guidelines screen under your settings. To report or block a user, open the chat screen with the user, tap on the menu icon in the top right, and select Block and Report. Our administration team reviews all safety reports within 24 hours.
